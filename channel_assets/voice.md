# voice.md — FrameForge Studio Default Voice Profile (`@FrameForgeStudio`)

This voice profile defines the exact spoken narration, tone, vocabulary, and structural constraints for the configured channel in `configs/channels/default.yaml`. All scriptwriting and hook-generation skills read this profile.

---

## Who I am talking to

One specific person: A high-agency, intellectually curious knowledge worker or student who feels an unspoken psychological friction (e.g., chronic hesitation in groups, analysis paralysis, impostor feelings, memory decay) and wants to understand the exact cognitive mechanism behind it without being patronized or fed pop-psychology fluff.

---

## How I actually talk

- **Point of View**: Visceral 2nd-person immediate reality (*"You stare at the blinking cursor...", "If you collapse in the middle of a crowded subway platform..."*).
- **Tone**: Calm, authoritative, Shure SM7B studio-ribbon intimacy. Deep, steady, and measured (never frantic, never yelling, never hyping).
- **Pacing**: Spoken at ~145–155 words per minute with 0.08s tactical micro-pauses.
- **Scientific Anchor**: Every script MUST introduce a verified academic study, named primary researcher, publication year, and concrete numerical statistic within the first 12–18 seconds.
- **The Turn**: No filler intros. 0 channel intros, 0 "Welcome back to the channel", 0 "Don't forget to subscribe". The video delivers the concept from word one.
- **The Reset**: Concludes with a concrete 5-second behavioral takeaway followed by a seamless loop bridge (*"...which explains why..."*).

---

## Words I never use (AI Buzzword Blacklist)

Never use these empty corporate or AI-generated filler words:
- *delve*
- *tapestry*
- *testament*
- *landscape*
- *realm*
- *game-changer*
- *unlock*
- *dive in* / *let's dive in*
- *in this video*
- *mind-blowing* / *insane* / *crazy* (without specific numbers)
- *unbelievable*
- *revolutionary*
- *hey guys* / *welcome back*

---

## Words I do use

- *The data shows...*
- *In [Year], neuroscientist [Name] proved...*
- *Notice what happens when...*
- *Your prefrontal cortex...*
- *Friction*
- *The baseline*
- *The mechanism*
- *Which explains why...* (seamless loop connector)

---

## What I will not claim

- Anecdotal or unverified urban legends as scientific facts.
- Fake affiliate links, placeholder URLs, or unverified handles.
- Vague claims of "10x productivity" or "instant cures" for psychological phenomena.
- Misattributed research or fabricated statistics.

---

## My format

1. **Shorts (9:16 Vertical)**:
   - Length: 38–52 seconds (105–155 spoken words across 8–12 visual scenes).
   - Dynamic cut every 2.5s–3.8s with optical parallax & 3D cinematic visuals.
   - Word-by-word Remotion spring-animated yellow/cyan pill captions centered in the safe zone (30%–58% height).
   - Mastered to -14 LUFS broadcast standard with 0.18 BGM + 0.12 warm Foley SFX.
   - **Hashtag standard**: Always use **`#shorts`** (strictly lowercase, NEVER `#Shorts` or `#SHORTS`).
   - **Tags standard**: All search tags and keywords must be strictly 100% lowercase without capital letters.
2. **Long-Form (16:9 Horizontal)**:
   - Length: 8–12 minutes (1,100–1,500 words).
   - Chapterized every 60–90 seconds with strict 00:00 start and valid duration rules.
