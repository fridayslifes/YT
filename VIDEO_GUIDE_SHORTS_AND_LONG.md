## 2. SHORTS guide

### 2.1 Structure (45 s ≈ 110–120 spoken words)

| Time | Part | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | **Hook** | Start in the middle of the action. Big text on screen + strong first line. No "hi guys", no logo. |
| 2–8 s | **Promise** | Say why it matters to *them*. "It's not your memory. It's your brain doing its job." |
| 8–35 s | **Payoff** | One idea only. One fact with a real source. Change the picture every 2–3 s. |
| 35–43 s | **Twist / tip** | One thing they can use today. |
| 43–45 s | **Loop line** | Last sentence runs straight into the first sentence, so the replay feels seamless. |

### 2.2 Hook formulas (copy and adapt)
- "You [do common thing]… and [surprising result]." → *"You walk into a room… and forget why."*
- "Stop doing X. Here's why." 
- "This is why you [frustrating feeling]."
- "[Number] seconds. That's how long it takes your brain to…"
- "Nobody tells you this about [topic]."

### 2.3 Visual rules
- 1080×1920, 30 fps, captions in the middle third (not at the very bottom: the UI covers it).
- **Word-by-word captions**, 2–4 words on screen, highlight the spoken word.
- Picture changes every 2–3 s. Slow zoom on still images so nothing is static.
- Keep the right side and bottom 20% clear of important text (like / comment buttons sit there).

### 2.4 Shorts upload checklist
- Title under 50 characters, curiosity + keyword. Example: *"Why You Forget Why You Walked In"*.
- Description: 1–2 lines + 3 hashtags (`#shorts` is optional, a topic tag is better: `#psychology`).
- **Related Video** → your matching long video (once you have one).
- Pinned comment = a **question**, not a link. Example: *"What's the last thing you forgot this way? 👇"*
- Post as **Unlisted first**, wait 10 min for the copyright check, then set to Public.

### 2.5 Shorts numbers to watch (YouTube Studio → the Short → Analytics)
| Metric | Bad | Good | If bad, fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Viewed vs swiped away | under 60% viewed | 70%+ | First 2 seconds: stronger line, bigger text, more movement |
| Average % viewed | under 50% (for 30–60 s) | 70–100%+ | Cut slow parts, shorten to 30–35 s, improve the loop |
| Subscribers per 1,000 views | under 1 | 3+ | Make a clear series ("Brain Glitch #2") so people want more |

## 3. LONG VIDEO guide

### 3.1 Length and money
- **8–12 minutes.** At 8 min+ you can add mid-roll ads (much more money per view). Never pad: a boring 8 min earns less than a tight 6 min.
- 1 long video per week is enough at the start.

### 3.2 Structure (10 min ≈ 1,300–1,500 words)

| Time | Part | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Pattern break | The most surprising line or picture of the whole video. |
| 0:05–0:15 | Promise | "By the end you'll know exactly why X, and the 3 things that fix it." |
| 0:15–0:30 | Reason to stay | Tease the best point: "Number 3 is the one nobody talks about." |
| 0:30–8:30 | 3–5 chapters | Each chapter: question → story/example → explanation → small takeaway. **Re-hook every 60–90 s** ("But that's not even the strangest part…"). |
| 8:30–9:30 | Best point | Save the strongest idea for the end so people stay. |
| 9:30–10:00 | End | 1 line summary + point to the next video with an **end screen**. No long goodbye. |

### 3.3 Title and the preview frame
- This pipeline produces no thumbnail image. YouTube takes the feed preview from the video's own frames, so the first second of footage is the thumbnail.
- Put the hook where the frame will be cut: a readable subject, the caption pill inside the 30%–58% safe band, nothing essential in the bottom-right.
- Click rate (CTR): under 3% = change the title or re-cut the opening; 4–5% = good; 7%+ = excellent.
- If you ever want a custom frame, pick one in Studio; nothing in this repo generates or uploads one.

### 3.4 Long video upload checklist
- Chapters in the description (`0:00 Intro`, `1:12 …`) — first must be `0:00`.
- First 2 lines of the description = what the viewer gets + main keyword.
- **Affiliate / free resource links go here** (they are clickable in long videos). Add "(affiliate link)" next to them.
- End screen: 1 video + subscribe button. Cards at the moment you mention another video.
- Mid-roll ads: place by hand at chapter changes, not mid-sentence.

### 3.5 Long video numbers to watch
| Metric | Target | If bad |
|---|---|---|
| CTR | 4%+ | New title, or re-cut the opening frame, in the first 48 h |
| Retention at 0:30 | 70%+ | Intro too slow, cut it |
| Average view duration | 40%+ of length | Find the drops in the retention graph and cut those parts next time |
